# PCL Executive Summary

**For**: Leadership, Investors, Strategic Partners
**Date**: January 17, 2026
**Status**: Foundation Phase Complete ✅

---

## The Problem

Enterprise AI adoption is blocked by:

1. **No Governance** – Who controls AI agents? What can they do?
2. **Security Gaps** – Prompt injection, data leaks, excessive agency
3. **Compliance Risk** – EU AI Act, ISO standards, GDPR requirements
4. **Vendor Lock-In** – Trapped in proprietary ecosystems
5. **No Auditability** – Can't prove what AI did or why

**Result**: AI remains in pilot purgatory. Enterprises can't deploy responsibly at scale.

---

## The Solution: PCL

**PCL (Persona Control Language)** = Terraform + OpenPolicyAgent for AI Systems

PCL is a **governance-first programming language** that makes AI:

- ✅ **Accountable** – Every action logged, traceable, auditable
- ✅ **Secure** – ISO 27001, OWASP LLM Top 10 compliance
- ✅ **Compliant** – EU AI Act ready, ISO 42001 aligned
- ✅ **Portable** – Run on OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Azure, local models
- ✅ **Governed** – Policy enforcement, human oversight, risk classification
